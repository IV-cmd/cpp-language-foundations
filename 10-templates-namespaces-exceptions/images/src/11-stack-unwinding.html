Propagation and stack unwinding
Call stack when the throw happens
readFile()
throws, owns Tracer "readFile"
processFile()
no catch, owns Tracer "processFile"
main()
has the matching catch
Real output
Step
enter processFile
enter readFile
leave readFile (destructor)
leave processFile (destructor)
caught in main: disk read failed
The exception moves up the call stack until it finds a handler. Each frame it leaves is destroyed, and the destructors of its local objects run. That's why RAII (Module 8) also cleans up on errors.
