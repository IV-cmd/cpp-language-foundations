Three copies of the same logic vs one template
Without templates
int getFirst(const std::vector<int>&)
double getFirst(const std::vector<double>&)
std::string getFirst(const std::vector<std::string>&)
Same body three times. A fix has to be made three times.
With a template
template <typename T>
T getFirst(const std::vector<T>&)
written once
The compiler writes the three versions for you, when they're used.
